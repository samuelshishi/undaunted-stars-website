Who We Are

Undaunted Stars Football Academy was founded in 2017 as Ace FC and rebranded in 2019. The academy focuses on recruiting, training and promoting young footballers across grassroots and youth age categories, while supporting their development.

Our Mission

To identify, recruit, train and develop young football talents, with a primary focus on grassroots and youth football from U-9 to U-20, while creating opportunities for talented players to progress towards professional football.

Our Vision

To build on our position as a leading grassroots and youth football academy in Benue State and become a recognised talent development centre producing professional footballers.

Shima Joseph Mson

Head Coach & Team Manager

About the Coach

Shima Joseph Mson is a football coach and team manager with a strong passion for grassroots football, youth development and the identification of emerging football talents. His football journey began as a player, representing several academies in Makurdi, most notably DreamFoot Football Academy, before moving to Lobi Stars FC's reserve team in 2017.

He began his coaching journey in 2017 as a player-coach with Ace FC and transitioned into full-time coaching in 2019 with Undaunted Stars Football Academy, where he continues to lead player development and team management. His coaching experience also includes a spell with DreamFoot Football Academy in 2020.

Coaching Qualifications
  • England FA Level 1 – Introduction to Football Coaching
  • FCAAN 'F' Certificate
  • British PlayMaker Licence
  • Introduction to First Aid and Safeguarding Children
Areas of Expertise

Youth Coaching, Tactical Development, Grassroots Development, Talent Identification and Team Management.

Coaching Philosophy
"Football is played with the brain; the legs are just tools with which we carry out the information already processed by the brain. Train a player's brain and you create a great genius on the football pitch."
Vision for the Academy

To identify, develop and place young talented footballers on the pathway to becoming skilled professionals.

Ayila Terhile David

Assistant Coach – Undaunted Stars FA

About the Assistant Coach

Ayila Terhile David is the Assistant Coach of Undaunted Stars Football Academy, bringing years of grassroots football experience, competitive playing experience and a strong commitment to player development.

His football journey began at the grassroots level. He represented Logo Rhythm FC (Redeemers) from 2007 to 2010, Eleven Stars FC from 2011 to 2013, and Ace FC from 2013 to 2018, where he served as team captain from 2014 to 2018.

In 2021, he became part of Undaunted Stars FA under the guidance of Head Coach Shima Joseph Mson. His playing positions included Right Wing Back, Left Wing Back, Centre Back and Defensive Midfielder. He also represented 159th Ben Coy, BHBC and BBC through The Boys' Brigade Nigeria.

Coaching Experience

Since 2023, Ayila has been actively involved in coaching and player development at Undaunted Stars FA, supporting training activities, tactical preparation and the overall development of the team.

Coaching Achievements
  • Undaunted Stars FA: 1 Gold, 1 Silver and 3 Bronze Medals.
  • Aggo College: 2 Silver and 2 Bronze Medals.
Playing Achievements
  • Logo Rhythm FC: 1 Gold Medal.
  • 159th Ben Coy, BHBC and BLBC: 5 Gold, 1 Silver and 1 Bronze Medal.
Coaching Philosophy
My coaching philosophy is built around commitment, discipline, preparation and a winning mentality. I believe in approaching every game like a final, respecting every opponent and taking things one game at a time.
Commitment to the Academy

As Assistant Coach, I support the Head Coach in training programmes, tactical preparation, match strategies, player development and the overall welfare of the academy.

I am committed to giving my TIME, TALENT and TREASURE towards the growth, success and continued progress of Undaunted Stars FA.
